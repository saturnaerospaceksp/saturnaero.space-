Launch Rules

Launch Commit Policy

Daphnis is GO only when the vehicle configuration matches the mission build, the tower handshake is current, the launch window is open, and the stack is free of stale state.

The launch controller must confirm that guidance, staging, telemetry, and vehicle arming all initialize cleanly before terminal count can continue.

Weather Criteria

GO weather means a clean sky and a stable pad environment. Thunderstorms need to stay well clear of the pad and the downwind track. The call is based on operator judgment with these limits:

  • No lightning in the launch corridor or downrange.
  • No thunderstorm cells within the local launch window.
  • Surface wind steady enough that the pad crew can track the vehicle safely.
  • No hazardous gust spread, wind shear, or crosswind spikes at liftoff.
  • Visibility clear enough for pad view, ascent tracking, and recovery monitoring.
  • Cloud ceiling high enough that the vehicle stays visible through first climb.
  • No heavy precipitation, icing, hail, dense fog, or active weather front over the pad.

Vehicle & Software Gates
  • The vehicle config checksum matches the active mission file.
  • The tower reports a current handoff and confirms vehicle identity.
  • All engine groups arm successfully and ignition health is nominal.
  • Fuel tanks, pressurization, and ullage state stay within bounds.
  • Guidance initializes without heading drift and locks the ascent profile.
  • Telemetry and flight-comms links remain green through terminal count.
  • The payload stack is secured and fairing closure is nominal.

Countdown & Tower Rules
  • The tower is the source of truth for launch timing.
  • Handoff must be live, current, and not stale.
  • The vehicle may not proceed if tower state is mismatched.
  • Any unresolved hold pauses the countdown until revalidation.
  • Countdown minimum is 00:00:30.
  • Handoff lead time is 120 s.

Liftoff & Abort Rules
  • Clamp release, pad clear, and liftoff thrust must all validate cleanly.
  • Pad clear altitude is 250 m.
  • Core ignition check occurs 0.5 s before launch.
  • Any stale stack data forces HOLD and a fresh recheck.
  • Abort is always available if the vehicle or tower loses confidence.
  • Unclear state is not launch state.
