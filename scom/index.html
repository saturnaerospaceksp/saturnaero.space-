SCOM Broadband Systems

Fast internet, wherever the mission goes.

SCOM keeps crews connected while they are on the move, out in the field, or far from normal coverage. It is meant for places where the link usually drops out, and for missions where having a steady connection just makes the work easier.

Version 6

SCOM v6 is the current build.

This is the sixth revision of the system. It is the one we settled on after the earlier versions taught us what actually mattered: better range, less fuss, and a link that stays useful when the mission gets busy. Compared with version 5, the new platform has about 15x the headroom we wanted in the first place.

SCOM v6 vs v5

The newer unit is easier to deploy and less sensitive to movement. It is a more practical piece of hardware, not a louder one.
